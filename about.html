> Started as a joke about being careful.

The name was a bit at first. Then we realized it was actually the whole pitch.

How we got here

Paranoid Droid started in a garage in 2011, fixing phones for friends who were sick of the mall kiosk guys poking through their photos "just to test the camera." We got a reputation for being a little overly careful — hence the name — and it turned out that's exactly what people wanted from someone holding their whole digital life in their hands.

Since then we've expanded into laptops, desktops, and game consoles, but the original rule hasn't changed: your device doesn't leave our sight, and your data doesn't leave your device.
